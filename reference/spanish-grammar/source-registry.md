# Spanish Grammar Source Registry

**Status:** Frozen for initial grammar construction  
**Registry version:** 1.0  
**Freeze date:** 2026-08-04  
**Applies to:** The Spanish grammar source ledger, descriptive entries, and downstream curriculum decisions

## 1. Purpose of the registry

This registry fixes the identity, authority, role, online location, citation form, and reuse boundary of every source approved for systematic grammar work. A source is not approved merely because it appears in a note or was returned by a search engine.

"Frozen" means that grammar work must cite the source records and versions below. It does not mean that a dynamic website has been copied or that its content will never change. For a dynamic source, every use must record its consultation date and the version displayed by the source, when one is available.

Adding a source, substituting an edition, changing a source's role, or accepting a changed online version requires a new registry version and an impact review. The review must identify any source-ledger rows and descriptive entries that may need to be checked again.

## 2. Authority hierarchy

| Rank | Source IDs | Authorised role | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `NGLE-2025`, `NGLE-ONLINE` | Primary authority for the coverage and description of Spanish morphology and syntax | `NGLE-2025` is the current revised edition; `NGLE-ONLINE` is the fully navigable working text. Their editions must not be conflated. |
| 2 | `NGB-2011-ONLINE`, `GTG-1.0-ONLINE` | Terminology, definitions, orientation, and navigation | May clarify or locate an analysis, but may not silently replace the NGLE or define coverage. |
| 3 | `PCIC-2006-ONLINE` | Pedagogical cross-reference | Identifies level inventories, communicative functions, and possible curriculum relevance; it is not a descriptive grammar authority or a ready-made lesson order. |
| 4 | `DLE-23.8.1`, `DE-ONLINE`, `DPD-2-PROVISIONAL-ONLINE` | Lexical properties, current senses, constructions recorded in dictionary entries, usage guidance, and variation | Lexicographical and normative evidence supplements grammatical description. Dictionary silence is not proof that a construction is impossible. |
| 5 | `ADESSE-3.24.10` | Verb valency and attested syntactic-semantic patterns | Corpus-derived research evidence only, subject to the licence gate in section 9. It does not determine the project's phrase-structure analysis. |
| 6 | `CORPES-1.5` | Frequency, register, medium, time, and geographical distribution | Empirical distributional evidence only. Corpus frequency does not by itself establish grammaticality or instructional priority. |

When sources differ, the descriptive entry must preserve the disagreement or difference in scope. It must not manufacture a single consensus. For morphology and syntax, the NGLE analysis has precedence in a source report; a project analysis that departs from it must be labelled as a derived interpretation.

## 3. Edition split within the primary authority

The two primary records are deliberately separate:

- `NGLE-2025` is the second, revised and expanded print edition. It is the current primary authority, but the full text is not available to this project through an identified official online edition.
- `NGLE-ONLINE` explicitly identifies itself as the 2009–2011 work. It is the primary working text for systematic extraction, stable section-level navigation, and exact online references.

The official sites examined for this freeze do not establish that `NGLE-ONLINE` contains the revisions of `NGLE-2025`. Therefore, an online citation must not be labelled as a citation to the 2025 edition.

Until lawful full-text access to `NGLE-2025` is obtained and its changes are checked, the coverage ledger may claim complete coverage of `NGLE-ONLINE`, but it must also display `NGLE-2025 comparison pending`. It must not claim unqualified coverage of the current NGLE edition.

This is a known source-access limitation, not permission to omit the revised edition. Access through purchase, a library, or another lawful channel may close the limitation without changing the edition record; the access state and resulting comparison review must still be recorded.

**Official edition record**

<https://www.rae.es/obras-academicas/gramatica/nueva-gramatica-de-la-lengua-espanola-edicion-revisada-y-ampliada>

**Frozen access state**

The official record was consulted on 2026-08-04. It identifies the edition and its structure but does not provide the full text. The project does not currently possess a registered full-text access route.

**Intended role**

- Current primary authority for morphology and syntax.

- Final authority for resolving changes introduced by the revised edition, once the relevant text can be lawfully consulted.

Full form:

**Intended role**

- Operational source for complete morphology-and-syntax ledger construction.

- Stable paragraph-level citation using numbered and lettered identifiers such as `15.4a`.

## 6. Pedagogical cross-reference

### 6.1 `PCIC-2006-ONLINE`

**Bibliographic identity**

Instituto Cervantes. *Plan curricular del Instituto Cervantes: Niveles de referencia para el español*. Three volumes: A1–A2, B1–B2, and C1–C2. Madrid: Instituto Cervantes and Biblioteca Nueva, 2006. ISBN 84-9742-615-0.

**Official online location**

<https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/>

**Frozen access state**

The complete electronic presentation was consulted on 2026-08-04. The site does not display a separate electronic edition or version number.

**Intended role**

- Cross-reference grammar topics against the A1–C2 inventories.
- Locate relevant communicative functions, pronunciation, pragmatic strategies, notions, genres, and learning content.
- Supply evidence for later pedagogical relevance decisions.
- Alert designers to prerequisites and cross-inventory relationships explicitly signalled by PCIC.

PCIC must not be used as:

- the authority for whether a Spanish construction exists or how it must be analysed;
- an automatic lesson sequence;
- proof that a form belongs in this particular Japanese-speaker, oral-first course at the level PCIC assigns it;
- a neutral-variety policy. PCIC states that it starts from north-central Peninsular Spanish while adding specifications for other varieties.

**Required citation**

Full work reference:

> Instituto Cervantes. *Plan curricular del Instituto Cervantes: Niveles de referencia para el español*. Madrid: Instituto Cervantes and Biblioteca Nueva, 2006, vol. [A1–A2/B1–B2/C1–C2], inventory [name], § [item]. Online at <[exact page URL]> [consulted YYYY-MM-DD].

Project short form:

> `PCIC-2006-ONLINE` [level], [inventory] § [item] ([exact URL]; consulted YYYY-MM-DD).

An unqualified citation such as `PCIC A1` is not precise enough.

Real Academia Española. *Corpus del Español del Siglo XXI* (CORPES XXI), version 1.5, June 2026.

**Official online locations**

## 9. Quotation, extraction, and reuse policy

This section is a conservative project policy, not legal advice. It does not declare that every use outside the policy is unlawful or that every use inside it is automatically lawful. Applicable law, contractual terms, and source-specific permissions still control.

### 9.1 Default rule for all sources

- Paraphrase grammatical and lexical information and cite the exact source location.
- Keep source reports distinct from project interpretations and pedagogical decisions.
- Quote only when the source's exact wording is necessary, and then use the minimum passage needed with full attribution.
- Do not copy tables, diagrams, entry sets, example collections, chapter summaries, or substantial connected passages into the repository.
- Do not use automated scraping, crawling, bulk downloading, or systematic extraction unless the source provides explicit permission that covers the intended use.
- Do not treat public web access as an open licence.
- Project-created examples must be independently composed and checked. A cosmetic alteration of a source example does not make it project-created.
- Before any public or commercial release containing source-derived material, perform a rights review for that release and obtain permission where required.

No fixed quotation allowance is created by this registry. The absence of a numerical limit does not permit cumulative copying across many entries.

### 9.2 RAE and ASALE web resources

The RAE legal notice states that rights are reserved, that access grants no intellectual-property licence, and that commercial reproduction, transformation, distribution, public communication, extraction, or reuse of all or part of the site or its contents requires prior written authorisation. It also imposes a restrictive linking policy.

Accordingly, for `NGLE-ONLINE`, `NGB-2011-ONLINE`, `GTG-1.0-ONLINE`, `DLE-23.8.1`, `DE-ONLINE`, `DPD-2-PROVISIONAL-ONLINE`, and `CORPES-1.5`:

- retain only bibliographic metadata, source identifiers, exact references, project paraphrases, and any strictly necessary minimal quotations;
- do not mirror source pages or store bulk source text in the repository;
- do not redistribute dictionary entries, grammar passages, corpus exports, or collections of concordance lines;
- use exact URLs inside internal research records for traceability, but re-check the RAE linking and reuse terms before publishing those links in a public or commercial product;
- seek permission or legal review before any use that could amount to commercial extraction or reuse.

RAE legal record frozen for this policy: <https://www.rae.es/aviso-legal>, consulted 2026-08-04.

### 9.3 Instituto Cervantes and PCIC

The PCIC site displays “all rights reserved” and no open-content licence was identified for the online work during this freeze. Therefore:

- use `PCIC-2006-ONLINE` for manual consultation, exact references, and original project paraphrases;
- do not reproduce its inventories, examples, tables, or substantial passages;
- do not scrape or transform the inventories into a project dataset;
- re-check the Instituto Cervantes terms and seek permission or legal review before public or commercial reuse beyond citation and independently written analysis.

Rights notice frozen for this policy: the footer of <https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/>, consulted 2026-08-04.

### 9.4 ADESSE licence gate

The ADESSE conditions authorise free use of data exclusively for non-profit research. They require publications using the data to name the project, website, and one or more team publications. They deny ownership or redistribution rights and prohibit the data from being sold or included in commercial dictionary, thesaurus, or similar works. Use also entails acceptance of the separate BDS conditions.

Consequently, `ADESSE-3.24.10` is registered but restricted:

- its public project description and documentation may be evaluated to plan research;
- no ADESSE query result, annotation, example set, or derived dataset may be incorporated into the grammar repository, application, or a commercial deliverable unless the intended use is confirmed to satisfy both the ADESSE and BDS conditions or separate permission is obtained;
- before the first data query used as evidence, record the project's non-profit/commercial status, review the current ADESSE and BDS terms, and record the approval decision;
- if clearance is not obtained, valency must be supported by the NGLE and registered lexical sources, and the missing ADESSE cross-check must remain visible as a limitation.

Licence record frozen for this policy: <https://adesse.uvigo.es/licencia.htm>, consulted 2026-08-04.

### 9.5 Corpus examples and third-party text

Concordance text may carry rights belonging to its original author or publisher in addition to database rights. Corpus examples must therefore be treated as located evidence, not reusable lesson copy. Record enough metadata to reproduce a query without copying the underlying passage. If an exact example is indispensable, clear its reuse separately or replace it with an independently composed and source-checked project example.

## 10. Citation and evidence rules for grammar entries

Every source-backed claim must include:

1. the stable source ID from this registry;
2. the smallest useful locator: paragraph, section, entry and sense, inventory item, or fully specified query;
3. the exact URL for an online source;
4. the consultation date for an online or dynamic source;
5. the source version when the source displays one;
6. a statement-type label: `source_report`, `derived_interpretation`, or `pedagogical_decision`.

When a claim combines sources, cite each source beside the part it supports. A citation at the end of a long mixed paragraph is not sufficient if it is unclear which source supports which claim.

Recommended internal pattern:

```text
[source_report]
Claim: ...
Evidence:
  - NGLE-ONLINE § 15.4a–c (exact URL; consulted 2026-08-04)
  - DPD-2-PROVISIONAL-ONLINE, "article", § 2 (exact URL; consulted 2026-08-04)
```

For a derived interpretation:

```text
[derived_interpretation]
Interpretation: ...
Derivation: This combines ... from source A with ... from source B.
Evidence:
  - ...
```

For a corpus claim, the saved query specification is part of the citation, not optional working material.

## 11. Version drift and source failure

Before relying on a dynamic source after the freeze date:

1. confirm that its displayed edition or version still matches this registry;
2. confirm that the exact cited page or query service is still available;
3. check whether the source's terms of use have changed;
4. record the new consultation date on the claim;
5. stop and open a registry-impact review if the edition, version, content structure, or reuse terms have materially changed.

If an exact URL disappears, preserve the citation as a historical reference and mark it `source_unavailable`; do not silently redirect the claim to a different edition. An archived copy may be used only if its provenance and lawful use are established and the registry is amended.

## 12. Sources not admitted by this freeze

The following are not evidence unless added through a registry revision:

- LLM output or model memory;
- general web pages, blogs, forums, and unsourced teaching materials;
- the NGLE manual or other abridgements not listed above;
- automatically generated corpus or dictionary aggregators;
- example sentences whose provenance and status are unknown;
- unregistered editions of an admitted work.

Such material may suggest a research question, but it cannot support a grammatical claim or close a ledger item.

## 13. Known limitations at freeze

- Full-text project access to `NGLE-2025` is not registered. The 2025 comparison remains mandatory and pending.
- `NGLE-ONLINE` is an official 2009–2011 text, not an established online rendering of the 2025 edition.
- `DE-ONLINE` exposes no online version identifier, so each citation depends on its consultation date.
- `PCIC-2006-ONLINE` exposes no separate electronic version identifier and no open licence was identified.
- `ADESSE-3.24.10` cannot be used as product evidence until its non-profit-only licence and the incorporated BDS conditions are satisfied or separate permission is obtained.
- RAE web and CORPES use remain subject to restrictive reuse and linking terms; public or commercial release requires renewed review.

These limitations must remain visible in planning and release reports. None may be converted into an implicit assumption by omitting it from later files.
